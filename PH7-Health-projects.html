CASE STUDIES
PH7 Health
2022
The Problem

PH7 Health's digital ecosystem was disjointed. Patients, doctors, and pharmacy services were siloed across fragmented interfaces, leading to user confusion, operational inefficiency, and missed consultations.

The Solution

I designed a cohesive, cross-platform experience by streamlining the web portals and mobile apps for both practitioners and patients. By implementing intuitive UI flows and frictionless navigation, the entire healthcare journey—from scheduling to prescriptions—became unified and effortless.

The Impact

The redesign drove faster consultations, seamless pharmacy coordination, and significantly higher patient engagement and satisfaction—effectively bridging the digital gap in modern healthcare delivery.

Live Platforms: ph7.health & App Store (Patient/Doctor)
Industry: Healthcare
